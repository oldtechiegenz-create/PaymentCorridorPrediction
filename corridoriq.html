Model & governance

How the recommendation is made, and who signs it off

A LightGBM ranking model scores every feasible corridor. Two companion LightGBM regressors predict time to credit and all-in cost. Hard rules for compliance, caps and calendars run before ranking, and a person certifies every payment before release.

Decision pipeline

IngestSWIFT gpi tracker events, pacs.008 / MT103 history, FX feed, holiday calendars, nostro balances, sanctions lists
Feature storeHop count, cut-off proximity, calendar exposure per bank, amount band, FX tier, STP history
Rules gateCaps, client eligibility, excluded banks, calendar conflicts, screening alerts
LightGBM rankerLambdaRank objective, weighted by the client's cost-versus-speed priority
Human certificationChecker verifies screening, disclosures and route; overrides need a reason
Release & learngpi tracking; actual time and cost return to training weekly
